# FreeVox8 Research Bridge

[FreeVox8](https://github.com/GareBear99/FreeVox8) is the spectral vocoder / ghost-resynthesis research direction inside the ecosystem.

## Research positioning

FreeVox8 can be positioned around:

- STFT / overlap-add processing
- spectral masking
- formant processing
- phase-vocoder direction
- ghost freeze / spectral texture capture
- MIDI-controlled carrier/resynthesis workflows
- pluginval and DAW validation
